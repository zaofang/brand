- Use the unmodified logo files to link to Zaofang or a Zaofang project, in articles, talks, package listings and similar.

- Change the drawings: no redrawing, stretching, rotating, outlining, adding effects, or re-spacing the letters.
- Recolour outside the supplied versions (on dark, on light, single colour).
- Use the names or marks for another project, product, fork or service, or in a way that suggests Zaofang made, sponsors or endorses something it did not.
- Combine the marks with other logos into a new mark, or use them in a domain name or social handle.

## Forks

A fork of a Zaofang project must take a new name and drop the marks. Credit the original in words: "forked from zaofang/yan".

Questions about a use not covered here: open an issue in `zaofang/brand`.
