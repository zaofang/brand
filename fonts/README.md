# Fonts

All are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: free to use, embed and bundle, not to sell on their own.

| Role | Latin | Chinese pairing | Get it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display and long reading | Newsreader, 500 for headings, 400 for text | Noto Serif SC | [Newsreader](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader), [Noto Serif SC](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Serif+SC) |
| Interface | Geist, 400 and 500 | Noto Sans SC | [Geist](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist), [Noto Sans SC](https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+SC) |
| Code and terminal | DM Mono, 400 and 500 | Noto Sans Mono CJK SC | [DM Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Mono) |

The logos are drawings, not typed text, so they need none of these.

The stacks in `tokens/tokens.css` put the Chinese face second, so Latin text uses the Latin face and Chinese characters fall through to the matching Chinese one.
